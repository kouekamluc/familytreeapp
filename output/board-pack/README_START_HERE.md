# KKEVO Tech board pack - start here

Version 0.1. Drafted and sources checked 5 October 2026; final packaging 6 October 2026.
Classification: confidential board and counsel review. KKEVO Tech is not registered.

The eight editable Word documents form the working set. The combined PDF is the
42-page reading copy, with eight bookmarks and document-local page labels matching
the footers. It preserves the checked Word layouts. Bracketed fields deliberately
remain editable because the founders, capital, office, manager, vendors, contacts,
approval dates and authorization references have not been supplied or established.

## Recommended first actions

1. Read document 00 and hold the first founders' meeting using document 07.
2. Appoint a formation coordinator and Cameroon counsel; approve a quoted budget.
3. Decide the legal form, founders/share allocation, capital, office and manager.
   Document 01 uses a SARL as a working option, with French draft statutes and
   founder instruments. It must be replaced if another form is chosen.
4. Obtain the current CFCE/registry forms and requirements. Counsel completes and
   validates the dossier before the authorized representative signs and files it.
   Official identity, capital, occupancy, registration and tax evidence must come
   from the relevant people or authorities; no certificates are fabricated here.
5. Resolve the lawful family-data model using document 02 before real-data use:
   living relatives without accounts, children, deceased relatives, matching,
   sensitive inferences, overseas access and required permissions.
6. After incorporation, establish company ownership/custody of code and accounts;
   execute the applicable supplier/personnel/asset instruments in document 06.
7. Adopt the retention and operating procedures, appoint trained operators, and
   implement/verify actual deletion, consent evidence, support and recovery.
8. Complete the public notices in document 03 in both languages. Publish only
   approved, accurate user-facing sections on working public pages, with contacts
   and deployed behavior verified. Do not publish this confidential board pack.
9. Complete the release conditions and signed approval record in document 07.
   Assess each additional Central African country separately before expansion.

## What this pack does and does not establish

It supplies a formation plan, working instruments, proposed policies, procedures,
agreements, regulatory preparation templates and board records. No filing, email,
registration, trademark application, approval, signature or public launch has been
performed by creating these documents. All instruments require completion and
Cameroonian legal validation. Official forms and particular facts may require
additional attachments or documents; current requirements must be confirmed.

The word board describes an internal oversight group until valid legal powers
exist. For the SARL working option, it does not replace the manager or shareholder
decisions reserved by law. Proposed retention periods and service targets are
company choices awaiting approval, not claimed statutory periods or achieved
service promises. Registration alone does not authorize every data operation.

The app still needs release work described in the pack, especially actual erasure,
permission evidence, production hosting/mail, staffed contacts, supplier and
transfer clearance, signed physical-device validation and an off-device restore.
No app features or production deployment were changed as part of this document task.

## Record control

Keep the original proposed pack separate from adopted versions. Record legal review,
the approving body and its authority, decision reference, effective date, document
owner and next review. Extract and execute individual instruments with their
completed schedules; do not sign the entire booklet as a substitute. Store private
founder identities and raw case evidence separately from board circulation.

The official source links are in document 00 and the relevant documents. Authority
procedures, forms, fees, audit applicability and timing must be rechecked before use.
There is no claim that all Central African legal regimes have been assessed.
